Global Seasonal Climate Updates
The Global Seasonal Climate Update (GSCU) is WMO's flagship seasonal climate outlook, providing authoritative information on the current state of the climate system and expected temperature and rainfall patterns for the coming three months. Issued monthly, the GSCU brings together the latest global climate science to support informed decision-making worldwide.  
Learn more about the Global Seasonal Climate Updates
Learn more about El Niño / La Niña
Past releases
Since May 2019 the GSCU is being produced on a regular basis as a pre-operational product and disseminated widely to WMO Members, as well as regional and international partners and United Nations organizations. The GSCU current and past releases are accessible in real-time through a dedicated web page of LC-SPMME website.
